## LinkedIn post — English

You can have an idea, a useful skill and the motivation to start — and still be unsure who to build with.

StartBeside is taking shape in Melbourne to help people take that first step together.

The planned pilot is simple: meet people with different strengths, choose who you’d like to work with and try one small task together. Then decide whether you want to keep building.

You don’t need a finished startup idea. Bring a skill, a perspective and time you can realistically contribute.

## Short conversation prompts

1. What would you like to try building or learn by working with someone?
2. What contribution could you make to a small task over a short trial?
3. What time could you realistically commit, and when are you available?
4. What contribution would you want from another person?
5. What would make the trial useful, even if you decide not to continue together?

Treat the suggested small group and 14-day trial as planning options until confirmed. No guaranteed match, team, company, funding or investor introduction. Obtain permission before sharing a participant’s contact details with another person.
