# StartBeside: what it is, and Pilot 01

The site makes the promise on this page. Change this page first, then the site, never the other way round.

## What StartBeside is

A structured founder-discovery and venture-building process. It helps people who want to build something find the right people to work with, test a real problem together in a short time, and use evidence to decide whether that team and that idea are worth continuing.

What we sell is the process we run in person, not a list of people or a library of content.

The chain:

match → work together → customer evidence → review with experienced people → continue / pivot / pause → (for strong teams) a longer venture sprint

## The four questions

1. Can I really work with this person?
2. Is this problem real?
3. Is it worth continuing from here?
4. If we continue, what is the most sensible next step? (customers, product, revenue, an accelerator, or investment)

Finding out early that a team or an idea isn't right is a good outcome, not a failure.

## What it isn't

- **Not an investment fund or an investor-matching service.** The first goal isn't raising money. It's finding out whether there's a strong team and a real problem. For the right teams, investment is one later option among several: some go to an accelerator or angels, some bootstrap, some grow from customers.
- **Not a networking event.** It doesn't stop at introductions.
- **Not a consultancy.** The organiser runs the process and makes blockers visible. They don't build teams' products.
- **Not a software platform, yet.** The first product is a process run by hand. Once there's enough data and repetition, matching, follow-up and the mentor network can become software.
- **No fake scale.** The site should look serious, professional and able to grow, without showing numbers we don't have.

## Pilot 01: operating assumptions

These describe the first pilot, not the product. They can change after it.

- 12–18 people in Melbourne who can make a concrete contribution.
- 4–6 hours a week, alongside work or study. The entry point is low risk. Teams that choose to go further can later move to full time.
- An in-person evening first. People choose each other. The organiser suggests matches but doesn't assign teams. A team of two is fine, and so is finding no match.
- A 14-day trial on one small goal (for example, a few customer conversations and a simple prototype). Check-in on day 7.
- Day 14: the team comes back, reviews its evidence with experienced people, and decides to continue, pivot or pause. Strong teams can move on to a longer venture sprint.

## Boundaries for Pilot 01

- No need to quit your job or studies.
- No requirement to start a company.
- StartBeside takes no equity.
- No investment, funding or investor introductions are promised.
- Registering interest does not guarantee a place.

## Flow

Interest form → organiser review → short conversation → separate invitation → in-person evening → mutual choice → 14-day trial → day-14 evidence review → continue / pivot / pause.
