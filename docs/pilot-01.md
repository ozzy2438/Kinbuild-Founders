# StartBeside Pilot 01: the promise

The site makes this promise. Change this page first, then the site, never the other way round.

## In one paragraph

We are bringing together 12–18 people in Melbourne who want to try building something, can make a concrete contribution and can set aside 4–6 hours a week. We meet in person first. People who mutually choose to work together then run a 14-day trial on one small project. At the end, each team looks at three things: how well they worked together, what they learned about the problem, and what their next step is.

## Boundaries

- No need to quit your job or studies.
- No requirement to start a company.
- StartBeside takes no equity.
- No investment, funding or investor introductions are promised.
- Registering interest does not guarantee a place.

## The three questions the pilot answers

| Question | How the pilot tests it |
|---|---|
| Can I work with this person? | Complete one small task together |
| Is the problem real? | Talk to potential customers |
| Should we keep going? | Decide on what the evidence shows |

Finding out early that a team or an idea isn't right is a good outcome, not a failure.

## The flow

Interest form → organiser review → short conversation → separate invitation → in-person evening → mutual choice → 14-day trial (check-in on day 7, wrap-up on day 14) → decision.

The organiser suggests matches; people choose each other. The organiser runs the process and makes blockers visible. They don't build each team's product.

## Still to confirm before announcing a date
